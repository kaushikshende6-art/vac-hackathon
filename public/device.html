🔌 Hardware Pin Connections & Circuit Matrix
Component ESP8266 Pin Protocol Function
NEO-6M GPS TX D6 (GPIO 12) UART (SoftwareSerial 9600) Receives NMEA sentences ($GPRMC, $GPGGA)
NEO-6M GPS RX D5 (GPIO 14) UART GPS configuration
MPU6050 SDA & LCD SDA D2 (GPIO 4) I2C Data Bus Motion sensor & LCD display data line
MPU6050 SCL & LCD SCL D1 (GPIO 5) I2C Clock Bus Motion sensor & LCD clock synchronization
Physical SOS Button D7 (GPIO 13) Digital Input (Pull-Up) Emergency interrupt signal trigger
Active Buzzer D8 (GPIO 15) Digital Output (PWM) Audible alarm on SOS or Geofence breach
Green LED (Power/Fix) D3 (GPIO 0) Digital Output Solid when Wi-Fi connected, pulses on GPS fix
Red LED (Alarm/SOS) D4 (GPIO 2) Digital Output Flashes rapidly during SOS emergency state
